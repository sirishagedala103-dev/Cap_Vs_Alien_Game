# Cap_Vs_Alien_Game
Captain vs. the Alien! Help Cap survive an alien invasion! Move around the arena, shoot the alien three times to take it down and score 50 points. The alien will pursue you and fire at you so use E to raise your shield and send the alien's shots back. Pick up med kits to restore your health and see how long you can go before you're finished, all the while trying to beat your best time.
